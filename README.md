## THE STORY

**SECTOR 48. 1982. THE CITY BELONGS TO THE GRID SYNDICATE.**

You've just boosted an experimental crimson **Z80-GT Turbo Interceptor** off Grand Boulevard—and the underground radio is already crackling with six back-to-back syndicate jobs.

To bring down the Grid Syndicate from the inside, you must tear across the entire 32×32 city on a razor-tight clock: **collect hot contraband from drop-points across town, then deliver it to the next contact before the deadline hits `00`!**

No floating arrows. No hand-holding. Just your **monochrome satellite city radar** (steady dot for your stolen car, pulsing `+` for your target), your compass heading, and 208 km/h of pure 8-bit adrenaline.

**Miss a deadline? You're hauled straight back to your previous checkpoint to run the job again!**

---

## THE 6-CHAPTER CRIME SPREE (12 TIMED JOBS!)

1. **CHAPTER 1 — THE LEAK**: Collect the **Data Tape** from the dock spy at **Harbor Plaza**, then race it to **Press HQ** before the Syndicate buries the story!
2. **CHAPTER 2 — THE KEYCARD**: Grab the **Vault Key** from a fixer on the **Neon Strip** and drop it off at the **Park Gazebo** in Central Park!
3. **CHAPTER 3 — TURBO NITRO**: Boost a **Nitro Cell** from the pit crew at **Speedway North** and haul it across town to **Old Town Bay**!
4. **CHAPTER 4 — THE Z80 CHIP**: Snatch the **Grid Override Chip** off **North Blvd** and get it decoded at the **Hacker Den** on East Avenue!
5. **CHAPTER 5 — THE EMP CORE**: Pick up a smuggled **EMP Core** at **NW Highway** and sprint all the way to **Stadium South** to arm the relay!
6. **CHAPTER 6 — FINAL RUN**: Collect the **Master Key** from **North Ring** and make a 30-second mad dash down Grand Boulevard to **Spire HQ** to shut down the Syndicate Grid forever!

### SCORING & DEADLINES
- Completing any pickup or delivery saves your **safe respawn checkpoint** and awards **`150` points + `8 × seconds remaining`**!
- Watch the top **`TIME:XX`** clock—when it hits `10s`, it flashes **BRIGHT RED** and beeps through the final 5 seconds.
- If **`TIME:00`** runs out, you fail the run and respawn at your **previous checkpoint** with a fresh timer!

## HINTS & TIPS FROM THE PROGRAMMERS

- **Orient with `H:` and `N=UP`** — The monochrome map is fixed North-up (`N=UP`). Check `H:N/E/S/W` beside your Gear indicator to know which direction you're pointing on the radar!
- **Plan your route on the Radio Dispatch screen** — The deadline clock stays paused while the Radio Dispatch box is up, and your monochrome map is already pulsing the target `+` so you can plot your escape before hitting Gas!
- **Feather `SPACE` for hairpin drifts** — Tapping handbrake boosts turn rate so you can whip into narrow Neon Strip side streets without losing momentum.
- **Avoid Central Park turf when the clock is low** — Driving off-road across green park grass caps your top speed at 128 km/h instead of 208 km/h.

## LOADING INSTRUCTIONS

### On Real 48K ZX Spectrum / Tape Loader
1. Insert **SIDE A** (`build/game.tap`) into your cassette recorder.
2. Type `LOAD ""` and press **`ENTER`**.
3. Press **`PLAY`**. Loads and auto-starts automatically (`10 CLEAR 32767 : LOAD "" CODE : RANDOMIZE USR 32768`).

### Play in Any Browser (Web / GitHub Pages / Netlify)
The self-contained [web/](web/) directory bundles `gta82.tap`, [web/index.html](web/index.html), and the WebAssembly **JSSpeccy 3.2** ZX Spectrum 48K emulator (`web/jsspeccy/`). Publish `web/` to any static host, or test locally:
```bash
make serve        # Serves web/ at http://localhost:8000
```

### Instant Launch in ZEsarUX (Build System)
```bash
make run          # Assemble and boot build/game.sna directly in ZEsarUX
make run-tap      # Test the authentic BASIC autostart tape (build/game.tap)
make build        # Assemble and sync web/gta82.tap
```
